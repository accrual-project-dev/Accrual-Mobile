# Accrual for iPhone — Support

For technical support, account questions, synchronization issues, or purchase restoration, email **[accrual.project.app@gmail.com](mailto:accrual.project.app@gmail.com)**.

Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, steps to reproduce the problem, and any error message. Do not send your password, Apple verification codes, or sensitive financial records. Redact private information from screenshots.

## Accounts and synchronization

Guest mode keeps your records on your device. Signing in enables account features and synchronization through Supabase. Accrual does not execute bank transfers, payments, or investment trades.

If records have not synchronized, keep the app and its data installed while requesting support. Logging out or deleting app data can remove records stored only on that device.

## Restore a purchase

Open Settings and the Accrual Pro screen, then select Restore Purchases. Use the Apple account that made the purchase. If access is not restored, contact support with the product name and error message; do not send payment card details.

## Delete your account

Open Settings, open your profile, and choose Delete Account & Wipe. Enter the requested confirmation. If you use Sign in with Apple, complete Apple authorization to revoke access and finish deletion. Canceling Apple authorization leaves your account intact.

Account deletion is permanent. Export any records you want to keep before confirming it.
